03ProblemRishi

The rider’s one job was squeezed or hidden.

Screen A / OTP

Squeezed

  • Four small ~44×53px boxes, near the minimum comfortable tap size.
  • They huddle in the left half of the card; the right half is empty.
  • Uneven internal spacing blurs the hierarchy.
Screen B / Choose a ride

Hidden

  • 5 of 7 options fully visible: the 6th is cut behind Book Ride, the 7th not shown.
  • Scroll the list and the selected ride slides under the header, while the button still says “Book Ride @ ₹69”.
  • A large empty area sits below the last card.

04InsightRishi

Same problem, two screens.

The insight

Each screen has one job. In both, the layout gave that job the leftovers.

So I set four rules before touching pixels:

i.The task owns the space

Whatever the rider came to do gets the visual weight.

ii.Never hide a choice

If an option is offered, show it whole.

iii.Keep the commitment in view

The selection and the button stay visible together.

iv.Systems over one-offs

Auto layout and one 16px rhythm make each fix repeatable.

Boxes grew from ~44×53 to 60×60px and now span the full card, 24px apart. A darker border marks the active box. One 16px padding system runs header to button, on a clean auto-layout stack:

06Solution B · Ride sheetRishi

All 7 rides, one view.

Your pick stays visible, because the sheet grows when you reach for the list.

What changed

Instead of scrolling inside a fixed half-height panel, a drag on the list expands the sheet (Smart Animate, ~0.5s). The map shrinks from 284px to ~169px but stays as context. Cards get a soft 0 1px 4px shadow at 4% and a neutral tile behind each vehicle.

Before · fixed sheet, inner scroll
After · sheet expands on drag
Problems
  • A6th option half-cut behind Book Ride; XL Premium not shown.
  • BAfter scrolling, the selected Auto hides under the header.
  • CEmpty space under the last card while the list is cramped.
Fixes · why riders benefit
  • 1Sheet expands, map shrinks: compare every price at once, without scrolling from memory.
  • 2Selected ride stays in view, so the button always matches what you see.
  • 3XL Premium, the last option, is fully visible; no choice is buried.
  • 4Soft elevation and neutral tiles give the list clear card edges.
